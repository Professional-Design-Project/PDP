## 1. Background (what these texts must fit)

Decisions taken in the Discovery phase:

- The app is a voice practice partner (job interviews, thesis defence). Users talk to an AI interviewer and get feedback after the session, based on the transcript.
- Under Article 50 of the EU AI Act, users must be told they are talking to an AI unless this is obvious.
- The app must not infer emotions from voice (prohibited in education and workplace settings).
- User data is collected and processed on the basis of consent given in the interface. Session logs and transcripts are key for further development of the product.
- Data will be sent to external providers, possibly outside the EU (for example in the USA). ElevenLabs keeps audio and text by default; zero retention is Enterprise-only. Other providers' terms may be similar.
- Legal advice is still needed on consent wording, storage of voice data and transcripts, and transfers outside the EU (section 4).

### Notes for the UX designer

- Do not pre-tick boxes and do not hide the consents inside the Terms of Service. One checkbox per purpose.
- Proposed behaviour, to be confirmed by legal advice (question 3): boxes 1, 2 and 4 are needed to run a session at all, so a session cannot start without them. Box 3 (product improvement) is optional and the app works without it.
- The checkbox text must match the language of the interface. Keep the Polish and English versions in sync.
- Store a consent record for each user: which boxes, timestamp, language and version of the text. Version the texts whenever they change.
- Settings needs a place to see and withdraw each consent. Withdrawing must be as easy as giving it.
- Offer a link from checkbox 4 to a list of providers and their locations (to be prepared once the voice stack is chosen).
- The AI notice (section 2) is not a consent and needs no checkbox, but it must be shown before the first session and be visible during it.

## 4. Questions for legal advice

The priority questions are marked **(P1)**. We would like those answered first.

### A. Consent wording

1. **(P1)** Is the wording of the four checkboxes specific and informed enough to count as valid consent under the GDPR? Does the information we show (what is collected, why, which providers, how long) have to be in the checkbox layer or can it sit in the Privacy Policy?
2. Are four separate purposes the right split, or must any of them be split further (for example voice recording vs transcription, or training vs analytics vs testing)?
3. **(P1)** Which consents can be mandatory to use the app? Is consent valid if refusing it means no access to the app, or should the core processing (recording, transcription, feedback) rely on another basis such as performance of a contract, with only product improvement based on consent?
4. Is consent to use data for product improvement and training "freely given" when the app is free or has a free tier?
5. What is the minimum age for users, and what rules apply to users under 18 (our users are students)?
6. What must we do when a user withdraws consent: stop future processing only, or also delete data already used for training or analytics?
7. Is the AI notice in section 2 sufficient under Article 50 of the EU AI Act, and does it need to be shown in a specific form or moment? Our source says Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026, so please confirm the date.
8. **(P1)** Does giving users scores and feedback on their practice answers make the app a high-risk AI system under the AI Act (education or recruitment categories), or is a practice tool outside them? Which other AI Act duties apply?
9. The AI Act bans emotion inference from voice in education and workplace settings. Do speech pace and filler-word metrics computed from audio risk being treated as emotion inference or as biometric analysis? Or is it enough that feedback relies on the transcript?

### B. Storing voice data and transcripts

10. **(P1)** Is a voice recording biometric data (Article 9 GDPR) in our case, where we do not identify users by voice? Does the answer change if a provider creates voiceprints or separates speakers?
11. Is it legally much simpler to store only transcripts and not audio? What does that change for consent and retention?
12. What retention periods are defensible for audio, transcripts and session logs, and for data that has become part of a training or test dataset? What happens on account deletion?
13. Transcripts may contain personal data of the user and of other people (employers, names, CV details, thesis topics). How should we handle this, and does allowing users to upload a CV or other documents change anything?
14. **(P1)** Who is the data controller for the MVP and pilot (team members, a company, a university)? Do we need a legal entity before real users test the app? What must the Privacy Policy and Terms contain?
15. Is a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) or a record of processing activities required at MVP stage?
16. Which data subject rights must we support from the start (access, export, deletion), and what security measures are expected for an MVP?
17. What disclaimers do we need about the accuracy of AI feedback?

### C. Transfer of data outside the EU

18. **(P1)** Which legal mechanism do we need for providers in the USA (for example EU–US Data Privacy Framework certification or standard contractual clauses)? Is user consent alone acceptable as the basis for regular transfers, or only for occasional ones?
19. What agreements do we need with each provider (data processing agreement)? Providers such as ElevenLabs keep audio and text by default and offer zero retention only on Enterprise plans. Can we accept these terms, and does default retention make the provider an independent controller?
20. If a provider uses data for its own model training, what must we tell users and can we opt out of it by contract?
21. Do we need to name each provider and its location in the consent text, and should users be able to choose EU-only processing?
22. Does using a different provider later (for example after the Polish speech, latency and cost tests) require new consents?
